Chest Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts an event's bazaar apricots with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the chests the apricots take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost an apricot, and the minutes the packing takes. It uses no named constants. The apricots, the chest size, the weekly use, the chest price and the packing speed are yours to decide and read off your own orders, shelves and invoices. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your apricots, your chests or your venue and does not know whether the apricots will really stay fresh, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a chest is worth its price or an apricot is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

The chests truth

Chests: the apricots divided by the apricots a chest, rounded up, with what the last chest holds.

The weeks truth

Weeks: the apricots divided by the apricots used a week, with the apricots left for the next week.

The cost truth

Cost: the chests times the chest price, to the cent, and spread over the apricots.

The time truth

Time: the apricots divided by the apricots packed a minute, rounded up, in minutes and in hours and minutes.

Chest Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
