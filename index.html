Chest Math

The 265 apricots at 14 apricots a chest take 19 chests, the last chest holding 13 apricots.

Boundary: arithmetic only Chest Math counts an event's bazaar apricots with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the chests the apricots take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost an apricot, and the minutes the packing takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your apricots, your chests or your venue and does not know whether the apricots will really stay fresh, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a chest is worth its price or an apricot is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 265 apricots, 14 apricots a chest, 20 apricots a week, $11.25 a chest and 4 apricots a minute.

The weeks truth

The 265 apricots at 20 apricots a week last 13 full weeks with 5 apricots left for the next week.

The cost truth

The 19 chests at $11.25 a chest come to $213.75, $0.81 an apricot.

The time truth

The 265 apricots at 4 apricots a minute take 67 minutes, 1 hour 7 minutes.
